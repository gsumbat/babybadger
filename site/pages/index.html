FAQ

Questions parents and sitters ask us

How do I invite my sitter?

Add your kids, then tap Invite your sitter. BabyBadger writes a text that you send from your own phone, with a link and a 6-digit code. She opens it, sees exactly what you’ll see, signs the monitoring notice, and she’s in.

Does my sitter pay anything?

No. Sitters use BabyBadger free, always. Your one family plan covers up to 4 family members and all your sitters.

What does my sitter see, and what can I see?

She sees her shifts with you, your house rules, the care plan and each kid’s needs. You see her location, trips, logs and photos only while she’s clocked in on your shift. Between shifts nobody sees her location, including us, and you never see the other families she works for.

Does my sitter have to agree?

Yes. Before her first shift she sees exactly what you’ll see, signs a monitoring notice, and allows location on her phone. She can stop working with a family at any time.

Can I find a new sitter on BabyBadger?

Not yet. Today BabyBadger is for the sitters you already know and trust. We’ll open finding new sitters later, neighborhood by neighborhood.

Is BabyBadger a babysitting agency?

No. BabyBadger is software for your family and the sitter you chose. We don’t employ, recommend, screen or check sitters. When you ask, she shares the cards and reports she has, with their dates, and you decide if they look good.

Do I need to buy a GPS tracker?

No. With BabyBadger’s kids’ app (coming soon), an old phone or tablet becomes your child’s tracker. You pair it with a code; no app store account needed for your child.

What if my child doesn’t have a phone?

That’s fine. During a shift you see the sitter’s location, which is where the kids are. If you want to see your child anytime, the kids’ app (coming soon) turns an old phone or tablet into their tracker.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep full access to the end of your billing period. Help alerts and messages never switch off, and your sitter keeps her free account.
