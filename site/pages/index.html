SAFETY AND PRIVACY

Watching over kids, not over sitters

Monitoring only works if everyone trusts it. So the rules are simple and visible to both sides.

{{img name=badger-teddy w=88 h=110 class="sec-badger m-only" alt=""}} FAQ

Questions parents and sitters ask us

Can I see my sitter’s location when she isn’t working?

No. Location turns on when she clocks in at your home and off the moment she clocks out. Between shifts, nobody sees it, including us.

Does my sitter have to agree?

Yes. Before her first shift she sees exactly what you’ll see, signs a monitoring notice, and allows location on her phone. She can stop working with a family at any time.

What if my child doesn’t have a phone?

That’s fine. During a shift you see the sitter’s location, which is where the kids are. A kid device is optional; an old phone or tablet on Wi-Fi works (the kids’ app is coming soon).

Do I need a new sitter, or can I use mine?

Use yours. Invite the sitter you already trust by text. Finding new sitters through BabyBadger is coming later.

How does paying the sitter work?

She sends invoices from approved hours. Pay her the way you already do, by Zelle, Venmo or cash, and she marks it paid. If she set up Stripe, you can also pay by bank or card in the app, or turn on autopay. Stripe is optional and its fee is on her side. Invoices and in-app payments are coming soon.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep full access to the end of your billing period. Help alerts and messages never switch off, and your sitter keeps her free account.
